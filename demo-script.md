## Individual App Scripts (short)

---

### New Patient Self-Registration

**[Click Registration on landing page]**

This is what a brand new patient sees — before they've ever been to the practice.

They enter their basic info, date of birth, contact details. The system asks for their insurance card — they can take a photo or type it in. AI reads the card automatically — it extracts the member ID, group number, and payer details without the patient having to type anything. Then it verifies eligibility in real time against the payer.

Then they book their first appointment, sign a HIPAA consent, and they're done. The practice has everything they need before that patient ever walks in.

No phone calls back and forth. No paper forms on arrival.

---

### Self Check-In

**[Click Self Check-In]**

This is the link a patient gets a day or two before their appointment.

They verify their identity with their date of birth, confirm their demographics, update any insurance changes. Then health history, current medications, reason for visit. Consents — they sign right on their phone. And finally, they see their balance and can pay before they arrive.

The whole thing takes about 4 minutes. When they walk in, the front desk sees them as fully pre-registered. No clipboard, no waiting at the desk to collect information.

---

### Kiosk Check-In

**[Click Arrive & Check-In on landing page]**

This is the iPad at the front desk — for patients who didn't pre-register, or just want to check in when they arrive.

They tap in their date of birth. The system pulls up their appointment. They confirm their info, review any outstanding balance, and sign consent if needed. The front desk gets notified automatically.

It takes under 2 minutes and the staff doesn't have to do anything. The patient handles it themselves.

---

### Post-Visit Follow-Up

**[Click Follow-Up on landing page]**

This goes out after the visit — automatically, via SMS.

The patient gets a short survey. How was the visit, how do they feel, any questions about their care plan. AI reads the open-ended responses and routes them automatically — if someone rates highly, they're prompted to leave a Google review. If they flag a concern or mention a side effect, it gets routed to staff to follow up. No one has to manually triage survey responses.

It also includes care instructions and any prescription reminders. Everything in one link, no app download needed.

---

### Patient Portal

**[Click Patient Portal on landing page]**

This is the ongoing relationship layer.

Patients can see upcoming appointments, request a new one, or switch to telehealth. They can message the practice directly. View their billing, make a payment, see past invoices.

Their documents and consents are all here. Health history, visit notes when shared by the provider.

It's the one place a patient can go for anything related to their care — without calling the front desk.

---

*Each of these apps is linked — anything a patient completes flows directly into the staff dashboard.*
