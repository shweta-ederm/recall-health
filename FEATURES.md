# Recall Health — Product Features

A patient intake and practice management platform for dermatology practices. Designed as a unified system covering the full patient journey, from self-registration through post-visit follow-up.

---

## Apps

### Staff Dashboard
Internal tool for front desk and clinical staff.

- **Check-In Queue** — Today's appointment list with real-time intake status per patient
- **Insurance E&B** — Eligibility and benefits status with deductible tracking per patient
- **Patient Registry** — Searchable patient list with demographics, insurance, and visit history
- **Schedule / Calendar** — Day view across 3 providers with color-coded appointments and live time indicator
- **Two-Way Messaging** — Inbox-style staff-to-patient messaging with conversation threads
- **Appointment Accelerator** — AI fills open slots from cancellations by matching waitlisted patients
- **Analytics & Reports** — Practice performance metrics and custom report builder
- **Form Builder** — Configure intake forms and consent documents
- **Settings & Integrations** — EHR connections (eDerm, ModMed, Epic), user management, notification cadences, branding

---

### Self Check-In
Mobile-optimized pre-visit flow sent via SMS link. No app download required.

- **Appointment Reminder** — Confirms upcoming visit details before starting intake
- **Identity Verification** — OTP-based phone verification
- **Demographics Review** — Pre-filled from EHR; patient confirms or edits
- **Insurance & Benefits** — Displays verified coverage card, AI-summarized benefits (deductible progress, co-pay, estimated due today), optional insurance card photo upload
- **Clinical History** — Symptom and condition questionnaire
- **Consents** — E-signature for HIPAA, treatment authorization, and financial responsibility
- **Payment** — Co-pay and balance collection via Apple Pay, Google Pay, or card

---

### New Patient Self-Registration
For brand new patients with no existing EHR record.

- Phone number verification
- Demographics and insurance entry
- Appointment slot booking

---

### Patient Portal
Full-featured health hub for ongoing patient engagement.

- **Overview** — Upcoming appointments, quick actions, recent activity
- **Appointments** — View, reschedule, or cancel upcoming visits
- **Health Summary** — Conditions, medications, allergies
- **Documents & Records** — Lab results, visit summaries, care plans
- **Billing & Payments** — Invoice history and online payment
- **Messages** — Inbox-style secure messaging with care team (provider, billing, front desk)
- **Profile & Settings** — Personal info, notification preferences, privacy and security

---

### Kiosk Self Check-In
In-office iPad experience for day-of check-in.

- Large-text touch UI optimized for iPad
- QR code scan or DOB-based identity lookup
- Instant check-in confirmation with estimated wait time

---

### Post-Visit Follow-Up
Automated outreach after the appointment.

- Patient satisfaction (CSAT) survey
- AI-generated personalized care instructions
- Review prompts for Google, Healthgrades, and ZocDoc
